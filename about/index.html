About the data

This portal is a directory of Bangladesh government organisations that publish job circulars. It is built from a research snapshot taken on 11 November 2026. It is not a live feed, and it is not affiliated with the Government of Bangladesh.

What is listed

  • 189 organisations — every organisation in the research that publishes (or is expected to publish) job circulars, grouped by ministry area.
  • 33 circulars — only the circulars the research actually captured. A circular missing from this list may still exist on the organisation’s own notice board.

Two rows in the underlying research are cross-references (Bangladesh Bank and the Economic Relations Division, each listed twice) and are not shown as separate directory entries — they point to the canonical entry instead.

What the verification labels mean

Every notice-board link carries a label describing how it was checked on 11 November 2026:

  • Verified live (15 links) — the notice page was actually opened and confirmed during the research.
  • Standard pattern — not individually opened (135 links) — the link follows the standard National Portal address (/site/view/notices). That pattern is the usual address for government portals, but the page for that specific organisation was not opened, so it may or may not list notices.
  • Not verified (39 links) — the link was not checked, or no notice-board link is known. Use the organisation’s homepage and its own Notice / Career menu.

Known negative findings

For two organisations the standard pattern address was checked and returned HTTP 404, so it is deliberately not linked:

  • National Board of Revenue (NBR) — use the homepage and its own Notice / Career menu.
  • Roads and Highways Department (RHD) — use the homepage’s own Notice menu.

These cases are flagged on the organisations’ detail pages. A few organisations have no verified homepage at all (for example the Armed Forces Division) — their pages say so rather than linking to an unverified address.

Circulars and full text

A circular’s full text appears on this site only after the periodic checker has extracted it from the original PDF. Until then the circular page shows the title, post count, deadline and application link captured in the research, and links to the original PDF where one was captured. Circular text is never written by hand and deadlines and post counts are never guessed.

What this site does not do

  • It does not check for new circulars in real time.
  • It does not guarantee that a pattern link works, or that a listed deadline is still current.
  • It does not take applications or payments. Applications go through the organisation’s own Teletalk portal or the Bangladesh Bank e-Recruitment site.

Before applying or paying any fee, always confirm the circular on the organisation’s own site or Teletalk portal.
